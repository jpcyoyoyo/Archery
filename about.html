About Archery Academy

Our story, mission, and commitment to the community

Our Mission

Growing the sport — one archer at a time

Archery Academy was founded with a simple belief: archery is for everyone. From curious beginners to seasoned competitors, we welcome all who want to learn, improve, and be part of a supportive community built around the sport.

Our certified instructors bring a wealth of experience and genuine enthusiasm to every session. We believe that great coaching goes beyond technique — it builds confidence, focus, and a lifelong love of archery.

Our Facilities

A space designed for learning

Our Seattle facility features a full indoor range with multiple lanes, suitable for all bow types including recurve and compound. We maintain all equipment to the highest standards and provide gear for students who don't yet own their own.

Safety is our top priority. Every session begins with a thorough safety briefing, and all participants are supervised by a certified instructor at all times.

Policies

What to know before you come

We ask all students to arrive a few minutes early, wear closed-toe shoes, and follow all range safety rules. Youth participants must have a waiver signed by a parent or guardian prior to their first session.

Equipment is provided for all beginner classes. Advanced students are welcome to bring their own gear. Please contact us if you have any questions about what to bring.
